# Gemini prompts used for My Little Rockstar art

Tips learned: start a **new Gemini chat for each image** and begin with "Generate an image:" — follow-up edits in a long
chat often fail. Wait for the full-size download to finish before leaving the chat.

## Venue backgrounds (assets/art/, originals in raw/)

All use this frame:

> Generate an image: wide 16:9 background for a cartoon rock music game. Painterly stylized game art, bold shapes, rich
> colorful lighting. Scene: **SCENE**. Front-on view, the empty stage floor fills the lower third, center clear.
> No people, no text, no logos.

## Character style reference (assets/art/character_style_sheet.jpg)

> Generate an image: a character model sheet of 6 ORIGINAL rock musician characters for a 2D cartoon game, standing in a
> row, full body, same height, front 3/4 view. Proportions: big exaggerated heads about one third of total height,
> angular stylized head shapes, compact but sturdy cartoon bodies with a visible torso, short solid arms and legs, small
> 4-finger hands, chunky shoes. Early-2000s TV cartoon style: thick bold black outlines, flat solid colors with one simple
> shadow tone, big expressive eyes with heavy lids, thick slab eyebrows, chunky geometric hair. Diverse genders including
> non-binary, skin tones, body types and hairstyles; rock outfits; instruments: electric guitar, bass, drumsticks,
> microphone, keytar, violin. Plain light grey background, no text.

The in-game characters are drawn by `assets/js/avatar.js` in this style so every part stays mix-and-match.
